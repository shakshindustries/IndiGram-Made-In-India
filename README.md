## 🌟 Welcome to IndiGram

IndiGram is designed to make personal communication simple, enjoyable, and private. Whether you are sending a quick text, recording a voice note with reactive soundwaves, sharing memorable albums, or broadcasting to a channel, IndiGram provides a seamless experience without distractions.

## 🚀 Getting Started: Account Setup

### Step 1: Sign In with Phone Number
1. Launch **IndiGram** on your device and tap **Get Started**.
2. Type your 10-digit Indian mobile number (`+91`).
3. Tap **Continue** to request an authentication code.
4. You will receive a 6-digit SMS OTP. Enter the code into the boxes. If entered correctly, you will be verified automatically.

### Step 2: Profile Customization
*For first-time users:*
1. **Full Name**: Enter your name as you would like friends and family to see it.
2. **Choose an @username**: IndiGram creates a clean suggested username automatically (e.g. `@rahul.sharma`), or you can type your own custom username. IndiGram will check availability in real time.
3. **Profile Avatar**: Tap the circular camera button to select a profile photo from your device's photo gallery.
4. Tap **Continue** to advance to account security.

### Step 3: Account Security & Email Verification
1. Enter your primary email address. This email protects your account against unauthorized access and ensures your chats and profile can be recovered.
2. Check your email inbox for a 6-digit verification code.
3. Enter the code into IndiGram to finalize your registration.
> 🛡️ **Privacy Assurance**: If you ever close or exit the app before completing registration, IndiGram will automatically cancel the incomplete session for your safety. You will never be left in an unprotected or partial state.

## 👥 Connecting with Friends & Contacts

### Address Book Discovery
- On the main **Chats** tab, tap the floating orange **`+` (New Chat)** button in the bottom-right corner.
- When prompted, grant permission to access your contacts.
- IndiGram instantly highlights friends and family in your address book who are already on the platform. Tap any name to start chatting right away.

### Global Username Search
- Don't have someone's phone number? You can find them by their unique IndiGram handle!
- Tap the **Search bar** at the top of the **New Chat** screen and type their `@username` (for example, `@priya_verma`).
- Tap their profile to open a chat.

### Chat Requests
- If you reach out to someone who is not in your phone contacts, IndiGram creates a friendly **Chat Request**.
- The recipient can preview your message and choose to **Accept** or **Decline** before sharing their online status or media.

## 💬 Mastering Chats & Messaging

### Text & Expressive Emojis
- Type in the chat bar at the bottom and tap the orange **Send arrow**.
- Express yourself with rich formatting, emojis, and instant replies.

### Giphy GIFs & Stickers
- Tap the **Emoji / Sticker icon** on the left side of the text box.
- Browse hundreds of trending GIFs and animated stickers to bring your conversations to life.

### Voice Notes with Live Waveform
Say it out loud with high-fidelity audio notes:
1. **Hold to Record**: Press and hold the **Microphone** icon. You will see live audio soundwave bars reacting to your voice.
2. **Hands-Free Lock**: While holding the mic, slide your thumb upward toward the lock icon. You can now release your finger and speak freely.
3. **Listen Before Sending**: Tap pause to listen back to your voice note. Watch the fluid wave bar track your playback smoothly.
4. **Send or Discard**: Tap the **Send** button to deliver your voice note, or tap the **Trash Can** icon to cancel and delete it.

### Message Reactions & Actions
- **Quick Heart**: Double-tap any message bubble to instantly react with a `❤️`.
- **Reaction Menu**: Press and hold (long-press) any message bubble to open the reaction bar (`❤️`, `😂`, `🔥`, `👍`, `🙏`, `🎉`).
- **Additional Options**: While holding a message, you can also:
  - **Copy**: Copies the text directly to your clipboard.
  - **Delete for Me**: Removes the message from your chat only.
  - **Delete for Everyone**: Permanently deletes the message for both you and the other person.

### Delivery Checkmarks Explained
- ⏱️ **Clock Icon**: Your message is being sent or queued in your offline outbox.
- ✓ **Single Grey Tick**: Your message has been sent to the server.
- ✓✓ **Double Grey Ticks**: Your message has arrived on the recipient's phone.
- ✓✓ **Double Blue Ticks**: The recipient has opened the chat and seen your message.

### Offline Messaging (Outbox Sync)
- Underground, on a flight, or in an area with poor signal? You can still compose and send messages!
- IndiGram queues your messages in an offline outbox and displays a clock icon.
- The very millisecond your device reconnects to Wi-Fi or mobile data, your messages are automatically delivered in the background.

## 📸 Sharing & Viewing Photos and Videos

### Sending Media & Documents
1. Tap the **Paperclip / Plus (+)** button next to the message input.
2. Choose your source:
   - **Photos & Videos**: Select multiple high-resolution photos or videos from your gallery.
   - **Camera**: Snap a fresh photo or record a video instantly.
   - **Document**: Share PDFs, notes, or files.
3. Add an optional caption to accompany your image.
4. Tap **Send**.

### The Full-Screen Media Viewer
Tapping any image or video in a chat opens IndiGram’s custom media viewer:
- **Distraction-Free Mode**: Tap once anywhere on the photo or video. The top header buttons and bottom carousel switcher smoothly slide away, giving you a full, immersive view of your photo. Tap once more to bring the controls back.
- **Thumbnail Carousel**: A sleek thumbnail bar sits at the bottom when multiple photos are shared. Flick or tap any thumbnail to jump between images instantly.
- **Pinch-to-Zoom**: Pinch with two fingers to zoom in and inspect fine details.
- **Save to Gallery**: Tap the download icon in the top right to save the photo directly into your phone’s photo album.
- **Share Outward**: Tap the share icon to forward the media to another application.

### External Sharing (From Other Apps)
- You can share photos, videos, or web links directly into IndiGram from Instagram, YouTube, your device gallery, or your web browser.
- Open your phone's native **Share** menu in any app and select **IndiGram**.
- Choose the contact or group you wish to share with, and IndiGram will deliver it immediately.

## ✨ 24-Hour Stories (Status Updates)

Share your daily moments with your friends:

### Posting a Story
1. Switch to the **Stories** tab on the Home screen.
2. Tap **My Status (+)** at the top.
3. Choose what to share:
   - Take a new photo or video with the camera.
   - Pick an existing photo/video from your device.
   - Create a **Text Story** with custom colored backgrounds and elegant typography.
4. Add a caption and tap **Share Story**.
5. Your story will be visible to your contacts for **24 hours**, after which it automatically expires.

### Watching Friends' Stories
- Any friend who has posted a story will display an illuminated saffron ring around their avatar on the Stories tab.
- Tap to watch. Stories progress automatically with segmented progress bars.
- Tap the right side of the screen to skip to the next story segment, or tap the left side to go back.
- Swipe down anytime to close the story viewer.

### Checking Who Viewed Your Story
- Open your own story from the Stories tab.
- Swipe upward from the bottom of the screen to reveal the live viewer list showing who viewed your status and when.

## 🔒 Privacy, Security & App Lock

IndiGram is built from the ground up with your privacy as the top priority.

### Fingerprint & Face ID App Lock
Keep your chats away from prying eyes:
1. Tap the **Settings** gear icon on the main screen.
2. Select **Privacy & Security** -> **App Lock**.
3. Toggle **Enable App Lock**.
4. Authenticate using your device's Fingerprint, Face ID, or system PIN.
5. Choose when IndiGram should lock:
   - **Immediately** (locks as soon as you leave the app).
   - **After 1 Minute**.
   - **After 15 Minutes**.

### Custom Private Nicknames
- Want to remember a friend by a specific name?
- Open their contact info page in IndiGram and set a **Custom Nickname**.
- This nickname is private to you and will never be seen by anyone else.

### Deleting Messages for Everyone
- Sent a message or photo by mistake?
- Long-press the message bubble and select **Delete for Everyone**.
- The message will be erased from both your device and the recipient's device.

### Session Protection Guarantee
- If you begin creating an account (entering phone number & OTP) but close or kill the app before finishing your profile and email verification, IndiGram automatically clears the session.
- When reopened, the app will never leave a half-created account open or resume where you left off without re-authenticating.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Is IndiGram free to use?**  
A: Yes! IndiGram is completely free to use for messaging, voice notes, media sharing, and channel broadcasts. All you need is an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data).

**Q: Can I use IndiGram without giving contact permissions?**  
A: Yes! While contact access helps you quickly find friends already using IndiGram, you can always search for any user worldwide using their unique `@username`.

**Q: Why does my voice note have a visual wave bar?**  
A: IndiGram uses reactive audio metering to give you visual feedback while speaking, ensuring your microphone is capturing your voice clearly.

**Q: How do I mute a chat or group that is too noisy?**  
A: Open the chat, tap the user or group name at the top to view details, and toggle **Mute Notifications**.

**Q: What happens if I switch to a new phone?**  
A: Simply download IndiGram on your new phone, enter your mobile number, verify the SMS code, and log back in. Your verified email address helps safeguard your account identity.
