## 🧭 App Layout & Navigation Overview

IndiGram features a modern, clean interface where all primary controls are easy to find and use.

### The Floating Bottom Capsule Navbar
At the bottom of the Home screen sits a centered, floating glassmorphic capsule pill navbar with 4 main tabs:
1. 💬 **Chats** (First tab): Direct messages, groups, search, and active conversations.
2. ⭕ **Stories** (Second tab): 24-hour disappearing photo, video, and text status updates.
3. 📢 **Channels** (Third tab): Public broadcast channels, creator updates, and community feeds.
4. ⚙️ **Settings** (Fourth tab): Profile management, privacy controls, biometric app lock, and chat preferences.

---

### Chats Tab Interface
When on the **Chats** tab:
- **Top Header**: IndiGram logo and branding.
- **Top Search & Action Row**:
  - **Search Bar** (Left & Center): Type to instantly filter chats, contacts, or message text.
  - **New Group Button** (Middle-Right): Orange gradient circular button displaying a people icon with a small `+` badge. Tapping this opens the group creation screen directly.
  - **New Chat Button** (Far-Right): Emerald green gradient circular button displaying a chat bubble icon with a small `+` badge. Tapping this opens your contact directory and username search to start a direct message.
- **Chats List**: Displays all active conversations sorted by most recent activity.
  - Tapping a conversation opens the chat room.
  - Tapping any user's circular avatar opens the **Avatar Quick View Modal**.

---

### Stories Tab Interface
When switching to the **Stories** tab:
- **Top Card ("My Status")**:
  - Displays your avatar with a segmented status ring.
  - On the right side of this card are two quick action buttons:
    - 📷 **Camera Button**: Tap to quickly snap a photo or video story.
    - ✏️ **Pencil Button**: Tap to write a text story with rich backgrounds.
- **Recent Updates**: Chronological list of active stories from your contacts.
- **Bottom-Right Floating Action Buttons (FAB)**:
  - ✏️ **Small Upper Button (Pencil Icon)**: Opens the full-screen text status composer.
  - 📷 **Large Lower Button (Camera Icon)**: Opens the camera story creator.

---

### Channels Tab Interface
When switching to the **Channels** tab:
- **Top Search & Action Row**:
  - **Search Bar** (Left & Center): Find public broadcast channels by keyword or category.
  - **Create Channel Button** (Middle-Right): Glossy circular button with a `+` icon to launch the channel creation modal.
  - **Join Channel Button** (Far-Right): Glossy circular button with a link icon to join any channel via an invite link.
- **Channels Feed**: View updates, announcements, and polls from channels you follow.

---

### Settings Tab Interface
To open Settings, tap the **Settings** tab (4th tab on the floating bottom navigation bar):
- **Profile Card**: Displays your photo, display name, `@username`, and registered mobile number.
- **Account Settings**: Security settings, registered email, and session management.
- **Privacy & Security**: Biometric / PIN App Lock, read receipts, and blocked accounts.
- **Chats & Media**: Wallpaper options, auto-download preferences, and cache clearing.
- **Log Out**: Safely sign out of your account.

## 👥 Connecting with Friends & Contacts

### Starting a New Chat (Green Button)
1. On the **Chats** tab, look at the top search bar row.
2. Tap the **Emerald Green Button** (`chatbubble-ellipses` with `+`) on the far right.
3. Your synced phone contacts who are on IndiGram will be listed.
4. Tap any contact name to launch their direct chat room immediately.

### Creating a New Group (Orange Button)
1. On the **Chats** tab, look at the top search bar row.
2. Tap the **Orange Gradient Button** (`people` with `+`) located next to the search input.
3. Select the contacts you wish to invite to the group.
4. Enter a group title, optionally set a group icon, and tap **Create Group**.

### Global Username Search
1. Tap either the main search bar on the **Chats** tab or open the **New Chat** screen.
2. Type any person's unique `@username` (for example, `@arjun_patel`).
3. Matching global users appear with their avatar and verification status. Tap to open their profile or message them directly.

### Avatar Quick View & Contact Details Flow
- **Quick Avatar Preview**: On the Chats list, tap any contact's circular avatar photo. A sleek pop-up card appears showing their enlarged profile photo, full name, and two action buttons:
  - 💬 **Chat Icon**: Opens their conversation immediately.
  - ℹ️ **Info Icon**: Opens their full **Contact Details Page**.
- **Contact Details Page**: Shows username, phone number, shared media grid, and notification options.
- **Send IndiGram**: Tap the prominent **"Send IndiGram"** pill button on the contact page:
  - If opened from the Home screen, it takes you directly into the chat room.
  - Pressing Back from the chat room smoothly returns you to the Home screen without getting stuck in navigation loops.

### Chat Requests
- When someone who is not in your phone contacts messages you for the first time, IndiGram places it under a clean **Chat Request** banner.
- You can read the message without triggering read receipts, then choose to **Accept** or **Decline**.

## 💬 Mastering Chats & Messaging

### Chat Input Bar Controls (Exact Layout)
Inside any chat room, the bottom input bar is laid out for speed and simplicity:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [+]  | Type a message...                        | [📷] [😊] [🎙️] OR [ 🚀 ]   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  ^                     ^                                      ^
  |                     |                                      |
Left (+):        Center: Multiline               Right: Dynamic Action Area
Attachment       Message Input                   - When empty: Camera, Stickers, Mic
Drawer                                           - When typing: Send Arrow (🚀)
```

1. **Left Side — Attachment Button (`+`)**:
   - A circular orange gradient button with a white `+` icon.
   - Tapping it rotates the icon to an `x` and slides open the 5-option media drawer.
2. **Center — Text Box**:
   - Clean multiline input box with the placeholder `Type a message...`.
3. **Right Side — Dynamic Action Area**:
   - **When no text is typed**, three buttons are displayed side-by-side:
     1. 📷 **Camera Button**: Orange gradient circle with a white camera icon. Opens the camera for photo/video capture.
     2. 😊 **Sticker / GIF Button**: Orange gradient circle with a white smiley face (`happy`) icon. Opens Giphy stickers and trending GIFs.
     3. 🎙️ **Microphone Button**: Orange gradient circle with a white mic icon. Press and hold to record a voice note with live soundwaves.
   - **When text is typed**, the three buttons smoothly collapse into a single:
     - 🚀 **Send Button**: Orange gradient circle with a white send arrow icon.

---

### Text & Expressive Emojis
- Type your message and tap the orange **Send Arrow** on the right.
- Messages send with fluid animations and optimistic local delivery.

### Giphy GIFs & Stickers (Smiley Button)
- Tap the **Smiley Face Button** on the right of the input bar (when text is empty).
- Search across millions of animated Giphy stickers and trending GIFs.
- Tap any GIF to send it instantly with high-frame-rate rendering.

### Camera Quick Capture (Camera Button)
- Tap the **Camera Button** on the right of the input bar (when text is empty).
- Quickly take a photo or hold to record a video clip, and send it directly into the chat.

### Fluid Voice Notes with Live Waveform (Mic Button)
1. **Hold to Record**: Press and hold the **Microphone Button** on the right side of the input bar. A dynamic soundwave bar appears that ripples in real-time based on your voice volume.
2. **Slide Up to Lock**: Slide your finger upward towards the lock icon to keep recording hands-free.
3. **Smooth Playback Preview**: You can pause and listen to your voice note before sending. The audio wave bar plays with a fluid progressive fill.
4. **Send or Discard**: Tap **Send** to deliver, or tap the **Trash Can** icon to discard.

---

### Attachment Menu Drawer (+ Button)
Tapping the **`+` Button** on the left of the input bar opens a sleek drawer with 5 categorized options:
- 🖼️ **Gallery** (Cyan): Select up to 30 high-resolution photos or videos from your phone.
- 📷 **Camera** (Blue): Take a picture or record video directly.
- 🎬 **Video** (Purple): Pick large video files up to 50MB.
- 📄 **Document** (Green): Send PDF, DOCX, ZIP, or text files up to 25MB.
- 🎵 **Audio** (Amber): Share audio files or recorded tracks up to 25MB.

---

### Fixed Date Timeline Header Pill
- As you scroll through message history, a pill-shaped date badge stays neatly fixed just beneath the chat header.
- It dynamically updates to show the date (*Today*, *Yesterday*, or specific dates like *15 September 2026*), keeping you oriented without cluttering the screen.

### Message Reactions & Management
- **Quick Heart**: Double-tap any message bubble to react with a `❤️`.
- **Reaction Picker**: Press and hold (long-press) any message to pick from emoji reactions (`❤️`, `😂`, `🔥`, `👍`, `🙏`, `🎉`).
- **Options Menu**: While long-pressing a message, you can:
  - **Copy**: Copies text to clipboard.
  - **Reply**: Quotes the message directly above your input bar.
  - **Delete for Me**: Removes the message locally from your device.
  - **Delete for Everyone**: Permanently deletes the message for both you and the recipient (available for messages you sent).

### Delivery Checkmarks Explained
- ⏱️ **Clock Icon**: Message is queued in your offline outbox and will send when connected.
- ✓ **Single Grey Tick**: Message has reached the IndiGram servers.
- ✓✓ **Double Grey Ticks**: Message has been delivered to the recipient's device.
- ✓✓ **Double Blue Ticks**: The recipient has opened and read your message.

### Offline Messaging (Outbox Sync)
- When your phone has weak or no network connectivity, you can continue typing and sending messages.
- IndiGram stores them safely in your offline outbox with a clock icon.
- As soon as your internet connection returns, outbox messages sync and deliver in order automatically.

## 📸 Sharing & Viewing Photos and Videos

### Clean Photo/Video Sharing (Real Captions Only)
1. Tap the **`+` Button** on the left of the input bar and select **Gallery**.
2. Select your desired photos or videos.
3. **No Unwanted Auto-Captions**: IndiGram only attaches a caption if you deliberately type one in the caption field. It never inserts automated generic text like "1 photo" or "album".
4. Tap **Send**.

### Sleek Full-Screen Media Viewer
Tapping any image or video in chat (or from a contact's media gallery) opens the high-performance media viewer:
- **Top Control Bar**:
  - Close button on the top-left.
  - Download to device gallery and external share buttons on the top-right.
- **Single-Tap Distraction-Free Mode**: Tap anywhere on the photo or video to smoothly hide the top header and bottom carousel for an uninterrupted, edge-to-edge view. Tap again to restore controls.
- **Bottom Album Carousel**: When browsing a multi-photo album, a thumbnail strip at the bottom lets you flick and tap between photos with animated transitions.
- **Pinch-to-Zoom**: Smooth multi-touch zoom allows you to inspect details up to 5x.

### External Sharing (From Other Apps)
- Send content directly to IndiGram from your web browser, YouTube, device photo gallery, or other apps using your phone's native **Share** menu.
- Pick IndiGram, select your chat or group, and send instantly.

## 🔒 Privacy, Security & App Lock

### Fingerprint & Face ID App Lock
Keep your chats secure even if someone else holds your phone:
1. Tap the **Settings** tab (4th tab on the floating bottom navbar).
2. Go to **Privacy & Security** -> **App Lock**.
3. Toggle on **Enable App Lock**.
4. Authenticate with your fingerprint, facial recognition, or device lock PIN.
5. Set your preferred auto-lock timeout:
   - **Immediately** (locks as soon as you leave the app).
   - **After 1 Minute**.
   - **After 15 Minutes**.

### Custom Private Nicknames
- You can assign custom private nicknames to any contact from their Contact Details page.
- Private nicknames are encrypted and stored solely on your device—no one else can ever see the nicknames you assign.

### Deleting Messages for Everyone
- Long-press any message you sent and select **Delete for Everyone**.
- The message content is permanently erased from both your device and the recipient's device.
